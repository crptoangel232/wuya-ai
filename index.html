Project 03 — Agriculture

Wuya

Tools for farmers who need decisions, not dashboards. Crop intelligence, field monitoring, and spoilage prevention for West African agriculture.

Source Portfolio

What it does

01

Crop Intelligence

Insights on crop health, planting windows, and yield predictions.

02

Field Monitoring

Track conditions, weather, and soil indicators in real time.

03

Pest Detection

Identify pests and disease early with treatment recommendations.

04

Yield Analytics

Harvest data that informs the next planting season.

05

Supply Chain

Connect farmers to buyers. Reduce post-harvest losses.
